# Public Trade Center pick ownership recovery

Candidate branch `codex/public-trade-picks-20260920`, baseline `80933bb`; worktree `/Users/jacobc/Projects/warroom-readiness-public-trade-picks`. Source is frozen for independent review; no deployment or canonical shared pin changed. This is a bounded pick-inventory repair, not a declaration that the Trade Center or suite is fully ready.

## Failure, intended behavior, and repair

The actual local `buildPicksByOwner` in baseline `js/trade-calc.js` created three years for every format, guessed roster versus user identifiers, silently retained original rights when recipient ownership was unknown, and ignored consumed draft slots. Its caller capped even a 16-round seasonal draft at seven rounds and converted failed draft/ownership loads to empty arrays. A foreign league's global draft and ownership state could therefore influence the current trade.

`node tests/trade-pick-baseline.cjs` extracts the actual pinned baseline functions and caller round expression. It reproduces seven failures: 48 redraft rights at the local builder, 21 through the capped caller (expected 16), 48 future rights after completed redraft (expected zero), full rights from missing ownership, original rights retained after unknown recipient, a foreign-league transfer, and a consumed current slot still offered. [Exact reproduction](evidence/trade-pick-caller-before.json).

The new `js/trade-pick-inventory.js` delegates remaining-right calculations to the reviewed canonical `App.buildPicksByOwner` contract. It translates roster keys to the Trade Center's owner keys without an identifier guess. It requires complete, unique known rosters, league/season/format context, and provider evidence. Sleeper ownership, draft list, details, and partial-draft picks load with explicit HTTP/shape/identity errors and a 20-second timeout. Unsupported provider IDs are not sent to Sleeper. The helper never writes the global active-league bridge, account data, or provider state.

The MFL path preserves its exact future allocations from `futureDraftPicks`, including years and rounds outside a generic three-year/four-round pattern. It never manufactures future MFL allocations or current picks from an absent board. Current board validation remains canonical; missing independently configured dimensions, truncated boards, failed future feeds, unknown owners and conflicting allocations stay incomplete. MFL requests use the selected league's explicit credential context; foreign league/year credentials fail before a provider call.

The Trade Center now refreshes this evidence on league/roster/refresh changes. Exact account bytes and a sticky cross-tab boundary reject old replies, including an A→B→A storage event. Context fingerprints prevent a ready result from being reused for another season, format, settings or owner mapping. A failed same-context refresh retains the last rows as explicitly stale, disables adding them, and pauses current deal grading. Existing selections remain visible with removal and retry recovery. A now-consumed or transferred selected right similarly pauses the grade instead of disappearing from the user's draft trade. Phone picks no longer use the unrelated global NFL calendar as a substitute for actual league draft status.

Player valuation, owner market-engine reverts, and the configured shared source/pin are unchanged. Existing dynasty rookie/future prices are retained. Seasonal and startup rights remain visible in the manual builder but are explicitly unpriced; they cannot enter dynasty-priced generated packages or produce a current grade. This is truthful missing-model handling, not completion of seasonal pick valuation.
