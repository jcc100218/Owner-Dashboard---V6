## Reproduction and resolution

- **High: unowned credential use.** Actual old header/room/mirror callers read the flat session API-key slot even after account B replaced account A. The low-level provider accepted an explicitly supplied key, so its in-flight fences could not establish the old key's owner. The public helper now obtains canonical owner-bound saved credentials for the exact selected league/season/franchise, rejects conflicting explicit card credentials, and captures account/connection/view before any request. Unknown legacy provenance remains preserved and requires reconnect. It does not adopt or copy a private key.
- **High: stale publication and wrong draft fallback.** Old callbacks could catch a context failure, select unrelated `S.drafts`, retag it for the displayed league and publish. Old mirrors chose the first returned draft when the selected unit was absent. The consumers now require exact mapped league/year/draft identity, never fall back to unowned global rows, and keep shared `S` writes/refreshes within its active league. Empty or failed optional feeds do not become unrelated completed drafts.
- **High: canceled/overlapping mirror work.** Stop/restart did not invalidate the old in-flight poll; overlapping intervals could share cursor state. Each run now has one request at a time, a generation/account/connection scope, and checks before publication. A stopped or replaced run cannot alter cursor, status, slots or picks. A per-run cleanup prevents an old React effect from stopping its replacement. The existing contiguous-pick reconciler is unchanged.
- **High: fresh work from an old mounted caller.** A low-level new call alone cannot know that the calling Command Center belongs to account A after B signs in. The actual caller captures its mounted account/view and passes the selected league and continuity check to the poller. A stale caller returns before stopping or starting another run.

Independent review found two additional consistency gaps and they are fixed: mutating the selected card during an awaited read now retires the scope, and same-owner `_platformCreds` with a conflicting league/year/team/key are rejected before transport. The fingerprint covers the mapper's selected IDs, season, team, draft pool/kind, format, team count and explicit connection. Checks remain sticky after an observed mismatch.

## Open gates and next steps

This is a bounded context/polling candidate, not full MFL readiness. Canonical optional draft failures still collapse to an empty list; consumers cannot yet distinguish a genuinely absent draft from all transport/malformed failures. The header has no dedicated outage/retry status. The canonical board's status/dimensions remain inferred from the returned slots, without proof against a complete configured board; MFL may delay published draft results. Range-based starter constraints and truthful transaction/future-pick coverage remain separate high-severity work. Command Center's broader initial/global board assembly, account-scoped save recovery and full private authenticated journey are not validated by these changes. Parent owns coordinated shared/public integration, deeper journey tests and release verification.
